World of Physics

A revolving door to every verified physics source on Earth and out to 1 light-day.

PhysAlts maps universities, national laboratories, private deep-tech startups, historical discoveries from 1800 BCE onward, and deep-space missions — sending you straight to primary official sources.

How it works

Global coverage Includes every region and small or emerging research nations alongside flagship facilities.
Verified links Built from OpenAlex, ROR v2, INSPIRE-HEP, NASA/ESA, and official institutional pages with automated audits.
Personal bookmarks Create an account to organize labs, milestones, missions, and open courses into folders with notes.

Open data & provenance

Every dataset is schema-validated (JSON Schema 2020-12) and hashed with SHA-256 into a public provenance ledger so anyone can audit or reuse the index.

Common questions

Why does PhysAlts link out instead of hosting articles?

PhysAlts is designed as a fast discovery hub — you find the institution, mission, or course here and go straight to the authoritative official source.

Do I need an account to explore?

No. Everything is free to explore immediately. Accounts are optional and only used to save bookmarks, folders, notes, and your academic stage.

Are mobile apps coming?

Yes. After the web release, native iOS and Android builds share the exact same verified database and account sync.
