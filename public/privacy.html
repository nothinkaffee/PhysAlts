PhysAlts Privacy Policy & Open Data Attribution

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

PhysAlts uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication solely to sync your saved physics shortlist and selected career stage across sessions. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID, or GPS location.

2. Third-Party Open Science APIs

When you search or open a facility card, your device makes direct read-only requests to public open-science registries: OpenAlex (CC0), Research Organization Registry ROR v2 (CC0), INSPIRE-HEP (CC0), Wikimedia REST API (CC-BY-SA), and Esri World Imagery map tiles.

3. Institutional Naming & Trademarks

University, laboratory, and observatory names are listed for factual educational directory purposes under nominative fair use. PhysAlts is an independent platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CERN, NASA, ESO, or any listed institution.
