1. Data Controller & Contact

PhysAlts (App ID: org.physalts.app) is operated as an independent open-science educational atlas. For privacy inquiries, GDPR data-subject requests, institutional data corrections, or takedown notices, contact the Data Controller at:

  • Privacy Contact Email: privacy@physalts.org (Placeholder contact — replace with your verified operator email prior to commercial/store release)
  • Response SLA: Within 30 calendar days in accordance with GDPR Article 12(3).

2. Data Minimization & What We Collect (GDPR Art. 5 & Art. 13)

PhysAlts is engineered under strict Data Minimization and Privacy by Design principles (GDPR Art. 25). You can explore the entire global physics atlas without creating an account or providing any personally identifiable information.

What We Store (Essential App State Only)

  • Anonymous Firebase UID: When optional cloud sync is enabled, Firebase Anonymous Authentication generates a random, non-identifying token solely to isolate your saved preferences.
  • Shortlist & Career Stage Preference: The IDs of physics hubs you bookmark and your selected academic stage (e.g., Undergrad, MSc, PhD, Postdoc), stored in your browser's localStorage and optionally synced to your owner-only Firestore profile document.
  • Optional Community Submissions: If you submit a public physics facility node, only the institutional metadata (name, coordinates, HTTPS URL, summary) is stored publicly — never your identity.

What We NEVER Collect

  • Zero PII: No name, email address, phone number, postal address, or government ID.
  • Zero GPS / Location Tracking: PhysAlts never requests device GPS or background location permissions.
  • Zero Advertising or Behavioral SDKs: No ad networks, cross-app trackers, IDFA/GAID access, or session-replay tools.
  • Zero Third-Party Tracking Cookies: No marketing, analytics, or cross-site cookies are set.

Retention Period: Local preferences remain on your device until you clear browser storage or use the in-app erase button. Optional anonymous cloud records persist only until you trigger erasure in-app or request deletion via email.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Performance of a Service Requested by You — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b): Storing your bookmarked shortlist and career-stage filter locally and syncing them under an anonymous UID is strictly necessary to provide the persistent shortlist feature you invoke.
  • Legitimate Interest — GDPR Art. 6(1)(f): Enforcing Firestore authentication tokens and schema validation rules to protect platform integrity, prevent spam/payload abuse, and maintain reliable public-data availability.

We do not perform automated decision-making or profiling (GDPR Art. 22), and we never sell or rent user data.

4. Your GDPR Rights (Articles 15, 16, 17 & 20)

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (and equivalent laws such as CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15) & Data Portability (Art. 20): Inspect and download a machine-readable JSON copy of all locally stored and cloud-synced preferences associated with your session.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Update your selected career stage or shortlist directly in the app at any time, or request correction of public institutional entries.
  • Right to Erasure / “Right to be Forgotten” (Art. 17): Permanently delete both your local device state and your cloud Firestore profile document at any time.
  • Right to Restrict Processing, Object & Lodge a Complaint: Contact us or file a complaint with your local Data Protection Supervisory Authority.
Self-Service In-App Privacy & Data Controls: Open the Privacy & Data Controls panel inside PhysAlts to:
• Export My Data (JSON) — instantaneously downloads your shortlist, career stage, and anonymous session metadata.
• Permanently Erase Local + Cloud Data — deletes your owner-scoped Firestore profile document (enforced by firestore.rules), wipes local storage, and resets your anonymous session in one click.

6. Institutional Naming, Trademarks & Corrections

University, national laboratory, observatory, and experiment names (such as CERN, NASA, ESO, LIGO, MIT, ETH Zurich, and others) are presented solely for factual, educational directory purposes under nominative fair use.

  • Zero Trademarked Logo Scraping: PhysAlts does not scrape, host, or redistribute protected institutional logos or crests.
  • Non-Affiliation Notice: PhysAlts is an independent open-science project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by CERN, NASA, ESO, or any listed university or laboratory.
  • Corrections & Takedown Requests: Institutional representatives wishing to update coordinates, URLs, program details, or request removal of a listing may email privacy@physalts.org (placeholder — see Section 1) or open an issue in the public PhysAlts repository.
Note for Self-Hosters & Forks: Before publishing a production deployment or mobile store listing, replace the placeholder email privacy@physalts.org with your live operational privacy contact and execute the Google Cloud / Firebase Data Processing Addendum (DPA) in your GCP Console.
