Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026. We collect the minimum data needed to run optional accounts and bookmarks — never ads, never GPS tracking, never data brokers.

1. What we collect

  • Guest browsing: Zero personal data. Bookmarks and academic stage stay strictly in your browser's local storage.
  • Optional accounts: Email address, display name, optional bio/research focus, saved bookmarks, folders, notes, and academic stage.
  • What we never collect: Precise GPS location, advertising IDs, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party behavioral analytics.

2. Security & password protection

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Accounts use salted cryptographic hashing (scrypt / PBKDF2 or Firebase Auth), signed session tokens, and strict owner-only database rules.

3. Your GDPR & CCPA rights

  • Export (Article 20): Open Bookmarks → Privacy & Tools → Export My Data to download a portable JSON copy immediately.
  • Erasure (Article 17): Open Bookmarks → Privacy & Tools → Delete account & data to permanently erase your account and bookmarks.
  • Cookies: Essential session cookies and local storage only. Zero marketing or third-party tracking cookies.

4. Open-data attribution

Institutional and scientific metadata is sourced from open registries including OpenAlex (CC0), ROR v2 (CC0), INSPIRE-HEP (CC0), arXiv / alphaXiv (via DataCite), Wikimedia (CC BY-SA), ESO / S. Brunier Milky Way Panorama (CC BY 4.0), NASA Blue Marble, and Esri World Imagery.
